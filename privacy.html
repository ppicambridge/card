Privacy notice

How we look after your information for the PPI Cambridge Card. Last updated 19 November 2026.

Who we are

PPI Cambridge (Cambridge Indonesia Association) is a not-for-profit community association for Indonesians in Cambridge. We are responsible for the information collected through the PPI Cambridge Card. Contact us at ppi.cambridge@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Members: full name, email address, age group, whether you are a student, working or family, your card serial number and a private card key, and when you registered.
  • Partner pack requests: email address, business name if you give it, and when you asked.
  • Partner applications: business details, business email address, the perk offered, and the name of the person who agreed to the terms.

Why we use it

  • To issue and show your member card, and to email you a link to it.
  • To count how many Indonesians live in Cambridge. We only ever share totals, such as "60% are students", never information about individuals.
  • To send the partner welcome pack and manage partnerships.

Our legal basis is your consent, which you give when you register or request the pack. For partners, we use the details to set up and manage the partnership you agree to.

Who can see it

Only a small number of PPI Cambridge committee admins. We never sell your information, and we never share members' details with partner outlets or anyone else.

Shown publicly: for partners only, the business name, category, area, perk, conditions, address and website or map link appear on the card page.

Where it is stored

Your information is stored in a Google Sheet on the PPI Cambridge Google account, and card emails are sent through Gmail. Google processes this data on our behalf and may store it outside the UK, under Google's own data protection safeguards. The card website is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not store your information.

How long we keep it

  • Members: until 12 months after your card expires, unless you renew.
  • Partner pack requests: 12 months.
  • Partners: for the length of the partnership, plus 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent at any time. Email ppi.cambridge@gmail.com and we will reply within one month. Deleting your information also cancels your card.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Age

The PPI Cambridge Card is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top of this page.
